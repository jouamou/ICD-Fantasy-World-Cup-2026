Editing predictions

Predict as early as you like and change your mind freely. Predictions lock automatically at kick-off — you can no longer edit a match once it starts. Once the official result is entered, your points are calculated automatically. Nothing to submit, everything saves as you go.

⚽

Goalscorers

Naming scorers is optional but it's where the big points are. Start typing and pick from the autocomplete list, or type any format — full name, last name only, or FIFA-style initials like J. Quiñones. Accents are optional too. You can name one scorer for each goal you predict, and every correct one earns the bonus.
