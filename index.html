The goal

Predict the outcome of all 72 group-stage matches of the FIFA World Cup 2026. The closer your prediction, the more points you score. The colleague at the top of the leaderboard when the tournament ends is the ICD champion.

⏱️

Editing predictions

Predict as early as you like and change your mind freely. Predictions lock automatically at kick-off — you can no longer edit a match once it starts. Once the official result is entered, your points are calculated automatically. Nothing to submit, everything saves as you go.

⚽

Goalscorers

Naming scorers is optional but it's where the big points are. Start typing and pick from the autocomplete list, or type any format — full name, last name only, or FIFA-style initials like J. Quiñones. Accents are optional too. Up to three scorers count per match for bonus points.
